## Hidden costs or upsells?

### Takeaway
The headline $4-$5 price is often the floor of the total cost, not the total: recurring hidden line items (paid backups 20-30% of compute, unbundled IPv4 €0.60, overage $0.01/GiB on transfer, renewal that can ~1.5-2x intro), monthly-limit bandwidth fairness clauses, and checkout add-ons can flip "true" budget and drive average 3-year cost 70-100% above the headline. The strongest mitigations are locked-rate hosts (Contabo, CloudBlast, OVH, RackNerd-with-prepay), hourly billing (Vultr, DO, CloudBlast), and using a YAML total-cost worksheet capped before purchase.
